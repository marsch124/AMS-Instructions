How This Works

What is AMS Instructions?

A quick-reference guide system using QR codes. Attach labeled QR codes to key locations in your RV (bed, steering wheel, water systems) and scan them to see step-by-step instructions.

Getting Started

  1. Create instructions: Go to Settings → Add New Instruction. Assign a 3-digit number (001-999) to each task.
  2. Generate QR codes: Use a free QR generator (qr-server.com) to create codes for each number (e.g., encode "001").
  3. Print & label: Print the codes and attach to Duro tape at relevant locations.
  4. Scan & follow: Open the app, tap Scan, point at a QR code, and follow the instructions.

Tips

  • Favorites: Tap ★ on any instruction to save it to your home screen for quick access.
  • Manual entry: If scanning fails, enter the 3-digit number manually.
  • Offline: All instructions are stored locally. Works without internet.
  • Dark mode: Perfect for bedside use at night.

Sample Use Cases

  • Bedside (001): Evening wind-down checklist
  • Driver's seat (002): Pre-drive vehicle check
  • Water tank (003): Filling & maintenance steps
  • Maintenance (004-009): Various RV systems
