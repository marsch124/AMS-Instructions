How This Works

What is AMS Instructions?

A quick-reference guide system using 3-digit numbers. Attach labeled cards with numbers (001-999) to key locations in your RV (bed, steering wheel, water systems) and scan or enter the number to see step-by-step instructions.

Getting Started

  1. Create instructions: Go to Settings → Add New Instruction. Assign a 3-digit number (001-999) to each task. Fill in title, steps, warnings, and any other details you need.
  2. Print the numbers: Create cards or labels with 3-digit numbers (001-999). You can print just the digits, or use a free QR generator (qr-server.com) to embed the number for backup scanning.
  3. Attach to locations: Use Duro tape or adhesive to attach number cards to the relevant locations in your RV (bed, steering wheel, water tank, etc).
  4. Scan & follow: Tap the pink scanner icon (🔢 crosshair) or go to Settings → View All Instructions, enter the 3-digit number, and follow the step-by-step guide.
  5. Backup your data: Go to Settings → Export as JSON to create a backup. On iPhone, tap Share → Save to Files or Notes to keep your instructions safe.

Tips

  • Share instructions: When viewing an instruction, tap the "📱 Share via SMS" button at the bottom to send a completion message via text with the task name and location.
  • Favorites: Tap ★ on any instruction to save it for quick access from the home screen.
  • Scanning a number: Point the box at the printed number and hold steady, or tap "📸 Capture & Scan" for a deliberate one-shot read. Good, even lighting helps a lot.
  • Manual entry: Can't scan the number card? Just enter the 3-digit number manually in the number input field — always fast and 100% reliable.
  • Export/Backup: Go to Settings → Export as JSON regularly to back up all your instructions. On iPhone, use Share to save to Files, Notes, or email.
  • Offline: Your instructions are stored locally and everything works offline, including manual number entry. Camera scanning needs internet the very first time to download its recognition engine (a few MB) — after that it works normally.
  • Dark mode: The app defaults to dark mode, perfect for bedside use at night. Custom pink accents help tasks pop even in low light.
  • Search: Use Settings → View All Instructions to search or browse all your tasks by category.

Sample Use Cases

  • Bedside (001): Evening wind-down checklist
  • Driver's seat (002): Pre-drive vehicle check
  • Water tank (003): Filling & maintenance steps
  • Maintenance (004-009): Various RV systems

v10.0 (2026-08-13) - REAL SCANNING & HOME SCREEN ICON FIX

Fixed: The home screen icon looked wrong, and scanning a number never actually worked — it silently always failed and fell back to nothing.

Cause: The home screen icon was an SVG, which iOS does not support for home screen icons — it was silently falling back to an auto-generated screenshot instead. Separately, the number-scanning code was a leftover placeholder that always returned "not found," so scanning could never succeed.

What changed:

  • 🎨 New organic, hand-drawn "#" icon shipped as a real PNG (not SVG) — this is what iOS actually uses for the home screen tile
  • 🔍 Scanning now uses real on-device text recognition (OCR) to read the 3-digit number from the camera
  • 📸 Added a "Capture & Scan" button for a reliable, deliberate scan instead of relying only on continuous auto-detection
  • 🧹 Removed the redundant "Seek the answer" caption below the scan box — status text now only appears when something is actually happening (loading, analyzing, or an error)
  • ⚠️ Scanning needs internet the first time to download the recognition engine (a few MB); manual number entry always works fully offline

v4.0 (2026-08-12) - HOME SCREEN FIX

Fixed: Saving the app to the iPhone home screen opened a 404 error page instead of the app.

Cause: The app is published in a sub-folder (/AMS-Instructions/), but every internal address pointed at the top level of the website instead. Opening the app in Safari worked because Safari already knew where you were; the home screen icon had only the (wrong) stored address to go on.

What changed:

  • App start address and boundary corrected to the /AMS-Instructions/ sub-folder
  • Both home screen quick actions (Scan, View All) re-pointed to the sub-folder
  • Offline file list and offline fallback page corrected to the sub-folder, so offline use works from the home screen icon
  • Offline storage version raised to v4 to clear the stale files from your phone
