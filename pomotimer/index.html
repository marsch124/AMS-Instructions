      How this works

      A session is a chain of phases

      Instead of one 25-minute countdown, a session walks you through the whole arc: Start-up (settle in, put the phone away), Preparation (decide what you will do), one or more Pomodoros (focused work) with Pauses in between, an optional Long break, and a Cool-down at the end (note what got done, tidy up). Each phase has its own colour, so a glance at the screen tells you where you are.

      Templates

      A template is a saved sequence of phases. The Templates tab lets you add, rename, reorder and time each phase, and pick an icon and colour. Add a Pomodoro block appends several Pomodoros with pauses in one go. Built-ins can be edited or deleted; Restore built-in templates above brings them back.

      Quick start

      On the Timer tab, tap any template card and it starts immediately. The template you used last is listed first. The Quick timer chips run one single Pomodoro of that length with no other phases.

      While a session runs

      The big button pauses and resumes. Skip jumps to the next phase, Prev restarts the current one (or goes back if you just started it). +1 / −1 min adjusts the running phase. The list under the controls shows every phase; tap one to jump straight to it. The bar shows how far the whole session has come and when it will end. Leaving the screen does not stop the timer; a Continue card on the Timer tab brings you back.

      Auto-start and alerts

      With Auto-start next phase on, phases flow into each other and you hear a chime at every change (a rising one for focus, a softer falling one for breaks). Off, the timer waits at the end of each phase until you tap Start next phase. This can be set per template. Vibration and notifications are optional, and the last three seconds can tick.

      Phone in your pocket?

      The countdown is based on the clock, so it stays right when the phone sleeps or you switch apps. But phones freeze web apps in the background, so chimes only play while the app is on screen. Turn on Keep screen awake for the intended experience. Notifications help on Android in the background; on iPhone they need the app installed to the Home Screen.

      History and your data

      Every finished or stopped session lands in History with the Pomodoros completed and the focus minutes. A Pomodoro counts once at least 80% of it was spent. Everything is stored on this device only. Export writes templates, history and settings to one file; Import reads it back, so you can move to a new phone.
